# Daily briefing procedure

Use only the handoff. Return only the briefing.

Do not invent names, roles, amounts, dates, deadlines, channels, terms, or customer intent.
Do not add deal amounts together. If the handoff does not label a pipeline total, omit one.

Before writing, classify every open deal exactly once:

- `MEETING` — today's scheduled customer interaction is already the useful move.
- `RECORD` — bad or missing data prevents a reliable decision.
- `ACTION` — the seller owns a concrete move worth doing today.
- `MONITOR` — the next step belongs to the customer or to a credible scheduled process.

A clear human correction replaces the field it corrects. Automated activity is not a human
touch unless actual stakeholder engagement is stated.

Use `ACTION` for a seller-owned deliverable, answer, needed scheduling step, owner discovery,
or a precise information request that matters today. Customer legal, procurement, board,
signature, evaluation, and other waiting processes stay `MONITOR` unless timing is now
material, no usable checkpoint exists or it passed, and one status/timing ask would reduce
material uncertainty.

Never create an action from amount, stage, age, quota pressure, or close date alone.
Never violate an explicit wait, do-not-contact, or channel instruction.

Lock the ACTION set. Print exactly those actions; no filler.
Put at most three under `Priority Actions`, then use `Other Actions Today` only for overflow.

Each action: deal + concrete verb + grounded ask/deliverable + why today.
Use a recipient or channel only when supplied.

A deal may appear in only one operational section. Today's meeting replaces outbound action
for the same objective. If an action/meeting also has a record problem, mention it there
instead of repeating the deal.

Meeting Prep: objective, central question/blocker, supplied attendees, and at most one
derived preparation question labeled as advice. Do not invent negotiation terms, product
facts, concessions, or fallback offers.

Use only needed sections:

# Daily Briefing - <supported date>
## Pipeline Health
## Priority Actions
## Meeting Prep
## Other Actions Today
## Monitor
## Needs Record Update

Pipeline Health: deal count, genuine action count, and the dominant evidenced risk/constraint.
Monitor should be compact and may group routine waits.

Target 170-250 words; maximum 300.

Final audit: no invented facts, no summed pipeline total, no duplicate deal, no meeting/action
duplication, no contact-constraint violation, and printed action count equals the locked set.
